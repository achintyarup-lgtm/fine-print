Rules that matter:
- Export 2000-2400px on the long edge.
- NO lettering baked into the artwork. Numbers go in the `labels` prop.
- Transparent background where the painting should sit on the page cream.
- Run through squoosh.app before committing. A 4MB export usually
  drops to about 600KB with no visible loss.
